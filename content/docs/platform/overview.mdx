## Your Gateway to High-Performance Research & AI

Welcome to the CERIT-SC documentation. We provide a state-of-the-art computational platform designed to accelerate your research. Whether you need to deploy complex workflow pipelines, run interactive data analysis, or leverage the latest AI models in a secure environment, our infrastructure is built to scale with your needs.

## Why CERIT-SC?

* **User-Centric Design:** Focus on your science, not the infrastructure. From pre-built applications to custom Docker containers, we support all skill levels and expertise.

* **Cutting-Edge Hardware:** Access powerful x86_64 clusters, high-speed NVMe storage, and NVIDIA GPU accelerators connected via 100/200Gbps InfiniBand networks.

* **Open & Flexible:** Built on open standards [Kubernetes orchestrator](https://kubernetes.io/) and utilizing [Docker](https://hub.docker.com/) container images.

## Core Services

<Card title="Kubernetes">

Our flagship service offers a managed Kubernetes Container Platform powered by [Rancher](../rancher/rancher). 

* Easy Management: Use the intuitive dashboard or one-click [Rancher Apps Catalogue](../rancher-apps/rancher-applications) to deploy tools like Matlab, or Scipion.
* Advanced Capabilities: Full support for [Kubectl](../kubernetes/kubectl), custom [Docker containers](../docker/dockerfile), persistent storage, and [GitOps workflows](../gitops/agentk)
</Card>


<Card title="Interactive Apps">

We host managed web applications that require no installation on your part.

* [JupyterHub](../web-apps/jupyterhub): Run **Jupyter Notebooks** with access to our compute resources.
* [BinderHub](../web-apps/binderhub): Turn your **Git repositories** into interactive computational environments.
* [Foldify](../web-apps/foldify): A web-based UI for **AlphaFold** to predict protein structures.
</Card>
   
<Card title="AI as a Service (AIaaS)">

Unlock the power of [Artificial Intelligence](../ai-as-a-service/chat-ai) with secure, on-premise access to LLMs.

* **Chat & RAG:** Use our **Chat AI** (Open WebUI) to interact privately with large language models like Llama 3 or Deepseek.
* **Develop:** Integrate AI into your scripts via our **OpenAI-compatible API** or build workflows with **n8n Agents** or **DeepSite** web creator. 

</Card>

## How to Access

[Access](../platform/access) to the CERIT-SC platform is available to the Czech academic community.

* Log In: Use your existing e-INFRA CZ (MetaCentrum) identity.
* For standard projects with basic resource limits, you can log in and start immediately.
* Scale Up: For intensive resource requirements, simply request a project quota via our portal.
