## Your Gateway to High-Performance Research & AI

Welcome to the CERIT-SC documentation. We provide a state-of-the-art computational platform designed to accelerate your research. Whether you need to deploy complex workflow pipelines, run interactive data analysis, or leverage the latest AI models in a secure environment, our infrastructure is built to scale with your needs.

## Why CERIT-SC?

* **User-Centric Design:** Focus on your science, not the infrastructure. From pre-built applications to custom Docker containers, we support all skill levels and expertise.

* **Cutting-Edge Hardware:** Access powerful x86_64 clusters, high-speed NVMe storage, and NVIDIA GPU accelerators connected via 100/200Gbps InfiniBand networks.

* **Open & Flexible:** Built on open standards [Kubernetes orchestrator](https://kubernetes.io/) and utilizing [Docker](https://hub.docker.com/) container images, ensuring portability and reproducibility of your scientific results.



## Core Services

### 1. Kubernetes Container Platform

Our flagship service offers a managed Kubernetes environment powered by [Rancher](../rancher/rancher). It provides the ultimate flexibility for running your applications.

* Easy Management: Use the intuitive dashboard or one-click [App Catalog](../rancher-apps/rancher-applications)to deploy tools like Matlab, RStudio, and Scipion.
* Advanced Capabilities: Full support for [Kubectl](../kubernetes/kubectl), custom [Docker containers](../docker/dockerfile), persistent storage, and [GitOps workflows](../gitops/agentk)



### 2. Interactive Computing

Need an environment for data exploration? We host managed web applications that require no installation on your part.

* [JupyterHub](../web-apps/jupyterhub): Run Jupyter Notebooks with access to our compute resources.
* [BinderHub](../web-apps/binderhub): Turn your Git repositories into interactive computational environments.
* [Foldify](../web-apps/foldify): A web-based UI for AlphaFold to predict protein structures.

   
### 3. AI as a Service (AIaaS) New!

Unlock the power of [Artificial Intelligence](../ai-as-a-service/chat-ai) with secure, on-premise access to state-of-the-art Large Language Models (LLMs).

* **Chat & RAG:** Use our **Chat AI** (Open WebUI) to interact privately with models like Llama 3, Deepseek, or Mistral, and chat with your own uploaded documents.
* **Develop:** Integrate AI into your scripts via our **OpenAI-compatible API** or build workflows with **n8n Agents** and **DeepSite** web/apps creator. 


## How to Access

Access to the CERIT-SC platform is available to the academic community.

* Log In: Use your existing e-INFRA CZ (MetaCentrum), Elixir AAI, or EGI AAI identity.
* For standard projects with basic resource limits, you can log in and start immediately.
* Scale Up: For intensive resource requirements, simply request a project quota via our portal.
