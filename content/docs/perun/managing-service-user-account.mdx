Service account management.

## Creating a Service User
<Callout type="warn" icon="⚠️">
For the specific needs of Data Care Department, please **AVOID** choosing a username that starts with `VO_`. For example, a service user account for backing up `VO_xyz` should have the login `xyz_backup`. It is also important to ensure that all usernames are in lowercase letters based on the US alphabet, without special characters (which may apear in some non-English languages).
</Callout>

First, log in to the Perun system. [Perun system](https://perun.einfra.cesnet.cz).

Once loggen in, select **Access management (1)** from the left menu. Then, either enter the **name of your VO (2)** or directly **click on your VO (3)** in the list below.

![perun](/img/data-care/perun/Creating_group0.png)

In the details of your VO, click on the **Service accounts (1)** tile.

![perun](/img/data-care/perun/Creating_service_user_account.png)

On the following page, click the **Create... (1)** button.

![perun](/img/data-care/perun/Creating_service_user_account1.png)

Next, fill in the `Account name` and `Email` fields. Finally, click the **Next (1)** button. 

![perun](/img/data-care/perun/Creating_service_user_account2.png)

Next, select the EINFRA `Namespace` from the drop-down menu and enter the login in the `Login` field. Then, check `Set password now` and enter the password in the `Password` field, making sure to confirm it in the `Confirm password` field. Finally, click the **Next (1)** button. 

![perun](/img/data-care/perun/Creating_service_user_account3.png)

After verifying the user with whom the service account will be associated, click the **Next (1)** button.

![perun](/img/data-care/perun/Creating_service_user_account4.png)

<Callout type="info" emoji="ℹ️">
**Associated users** <br/>
If needed, you can associate an additional user identity with the service account. For technical reasons, this approach is strongly recommended.
</Callout>

Please enter the name, surname, email, or login of the user you wish to associate with the created service account, then click on the **Search** button. Select the user from the search results and click **Add**.

Finally, click on the **Create (1)** button.

![perun](/img/data-care/perun/Creating_service_user_account5.png)

## Adding a Service User to a Group

The created service user must be added to an existing group

In the details of your VO, click on the **Groups (1)** tile.

![perun](/img/data-care/perun/Creating_group1.png)

The next step is to enter the `Search by name, id. description` field. Once the list of groups appears, you can select the desired group. 

![perun](/img/data-care/perun/Added_service_to_group3.png)

In the group details, click on the **Members (1)** tile.

![perun](/img/data-care/perun/Added_service_to_group4.png)

Then, click the **Add  (1)** button.

![perun](/img/data-care/perun/Added_service_to_group0.png)

The next step is to enter the `Search user by name login or email`. Once the service user appears in the list, you can select it. Finally, click on the **Add (1)** button.

![perun](/img/data-care/perun/Added_service_to_group1.png)

<Callout type="info" emoji="ℹ️">
All changes made in the Perun system will take effect within one hour. These changes are not applied immediately!
</Callout>
