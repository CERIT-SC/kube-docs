---
title: Extension of Membership in the VO
---

When a user requests an extension of their membership in the VO under your administration, you will receive an email with a structure similar to the example at the end of this page. the email will contain a link that redirects you to a webpage with detailed information about the user requesting the extension. Click on the link and log in to the Perun system, where you can Approve/Reject the user´s application.

To approve/reject the application manually, log in to the [Perun system](perun.einfra.cesnet.cz/login), select the virtual organization for which you wish to manage the users applications, and then click on the **Applications (1)** tile.

![perun](/img/data-care/perun/perun-approve-user1.png)

Next, you will see a list of all submitted applications and their status. You can click on the **particular application (1)**, that you wish to accept/reject. By hovering your cursor over the `Type` field, you will see whether the application is for `Initial` membership or `Extension`.

![perun](/img/data-care/perun/perun-approve-user2.png)

Finally, you will see the detailed view of the application, where you can click the **Approve/Reject (1)** button.

![perun](/img/data-care/perun/perun-approve-user3.png)

<Callout type="warn" icon="⚠️">
All changes made in the Perun system will be active in one hour. **Thus executed changes are not immediate!!!**.
</Callout>


An example of the email sent to the VO administrator when a new application is submitted is shown below.

```text

New application for VO Test was created by user: Albert, albert@einstein.cz / https://whoami.einstein.cz/idp/shibboleth.

Application detail with all user submitted data where you can approve / reject the application: https://perun.einfra.cesnet.cz/organizations/xyz/applications/1525/?

If there was any error during application creation, information follows:

--------------------------------------------------
Your Perun management team
```
