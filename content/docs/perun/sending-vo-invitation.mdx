---
title: Sending an Invitation to Join the VO
---

This howto demonstrates how to easily invite a user to join your Virtual
Organisation (VO). Note that this mechanism invites users to **apply for
membership**. Depending on VO setup, if the criteria for membership are
evaluated automatically, they are still evaluated. If manual membership
approval is set up, an application for manual approval will be created, VO
manager (or membership manager) notified and requested to evaluate the
application, etc.

Do not confuse sending invitations with sending “pre-approved invitations”,
those are available in some repository systems (technically speaking, those
are approved immediately after the application is created).

How to create invitations to join a virtual organisation

First, log in to the [Perun system](https://perun.einfra.cesnet.cz/).

Once logged, select **Access management (1)** from the left menu. You can then either enter the **name of your VO (2)** or **click directly on your VO (3)** from the list below.

![perun](/img/data-care/perun/perun-invite1.png)

In your VO details, click on the **User (1)** tile.

![perun](/img/data-care/perun/perun-invite2.png)

On the next page, click the **Invite (1)** button.

![perun](/img/data-care/perun/perun-invite3.png)

Next, fill in the `Name` and `Email` field, then select the preferred `Language` option. Finally, click the **Invite (1)** button. Once clicked the **Invite (1)** button, a notification will be sent to the specified email address.

![perun](/img/data-care/perun/perun-invite4.png)

Example of the notification is listed below.

```text
Dear user,

administrator of Virtual Organization (VO) VO_xyz invites you to use data space in this VO. To become a member of VO, you must fill in an application. You must authenticate yourself using following link:

Invitation URL

You can see your application state in "Applications" section at:

Applications URL

You will be notified by another mail once your application approved.

--------------------------------------------------
Administrator VO_xyz
du-support@cesnet.cz
https://docs.du.cesnet.cz/
```
