---
title: Adding  a VO Manager to the VO
---

This guide will show you how to add a VO manager to the VO under you administration. For the technical reasons, we strongly recommend having at least two VO administrators.

First, log in to the Perun system. [Perun system](https://perun.einfra.cesnet.cz).

Next, go to the **Access management (1)** section, and **search (2)** the the VO under your administration. Once the VO appears **in the list (3)**, click on the desired VO.

![perun](/img/data-care/perun/perun-add-admin1.png)

In the detailed view of the VO, click on **Advanced settings (1)** and then select the **Managers (2)** tile.

![perun](/img/data-care/perun/perun-add-admin2.png)

On the displayed page, click **Add (1)** button.

![perun](/img/data-care/perun/perun-add-admin3.png)

Next, **enter (1)** the name or email of the chosen administrator, then click the **Search (2)** button. **Select (3)** the desired user in the list, and, finally, click the **Add (4)** button.

![perun](/img/data-care/perun/perun-add-admin4.png)

If the administrator is successfully added, they should appear **in the list of existing administrators (1)**. The final step is to add the contact address for the newly added administrator by clicking on **Attributes (2)** option.

![perun](/img/data-care/perun/perun-add-admin5.png)

On the displayed page, you can add additional email addresses in the `To email addresses` **(1)** field. Afterward, click the **Save (2)** button.

![perun](/img/data-care/perun/perun-add-admin6.png)

<Callout type="info" emoji="ℹ️">
**Access for the new VO manager is activated immediately.**
</Callout>
