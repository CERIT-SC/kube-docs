---
title: Adding VO manager into VO
---

This manual enables you to add a VO manager to the VO under you administration. From the technical reason, we strongly recommend to have at least two VO administrators.

First you need to login into Perun system. [Perun system](https://perun.einfra.cesnet.cz).

Then in the section **Access management (1)** you need to **search (2)** the VO under your administration. Once it appears **in the list (3)** you can click on the desired VO.

![perun](/img/data-care/perun/perun-add-admin1.png)

In the detail view of the VO you can click on **Advanced settings (1)** and then click on the **Managers (2)** tile.

![perun](/img/data-care/perun/perun-add-admin2.png)

On the displayed page you can click on **Add (1)** button.

![perun](/img/data-care/perun/perun-add-admin3.png)

Then you can **type (1)** the name or email of your designed administrator and then click at the **Search (2)** button. Then you can **tick (3)** desired user in the list below. In the end you have to click on **Add (4)** button.

![perun](/img/data-care/perun/perun-add-admin4.png)

If you successfully add the administrator He/She should appear **in the list of existing administrators (1)**. The last step is to add the contact address of the newly added administrator, you have to click on **Attributes (2)** option.

![perun](/img/data-care/perun/perun-add-admin5.png)

In the displayed page you can add more email address to the field `To email addresses` **(1)**. Then you can click on the **Save (2)** button.

![perun](/img/data-care/perun/perun-add-admin6.png)

<Callout type="info" emoji="ℹ️">
**Access for new VO manager is immediately active.**
</Callout>
