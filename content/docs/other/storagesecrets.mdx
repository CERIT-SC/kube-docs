---
title: Storage Secrets
---

To connect your MetaCentrum home storage to applications or your own deployments, you need to generate specific objects. We provide a website to simplify this process, where you can generate SSH secrets for accessing storage servers or directly usable PVCs to access the common `brno-12` storage within our Kubernetes cluster.

Currently, you can create two types of objects:

- SSH keys for accessing SSHFS or connecting via SSH to our servers, saved as Kubernetes Secrets
- PVCs that represent your `brno-12` home storage and can be directly connected to applications

To create these objects, visit [https://storagesecrets.cloud.e-infra.cz/](https://storagesecrets.cloud.e-infra.cz/).

## Requirements before use

To use this service, you **must be part of the META kerberos realm**—simply put, you must have a MetaCentrum account.

<Callout type="warn">
In addition to being in the correct realm, you also need your kerberos name associated with your Rancher account. This association should happen automatically upon login to Rancher, but is a recent addition. You may need to log out and back into Rancher at [https://rancher.cloud.e-infra.cz](https://rancher.cloud.e-infra.cz/).

It may take up to 5 minutes for your kerberos name to be associated with your Rancher account, so please wait before trying to access the site again.
</Callout>

## Using the site

The site provides a form where you can select what type of object to create and which server to access.

At the bottom of the site is a namespace selector, where you should select a namespace where the generated objects will be saved. If you don't know what a namespace is or need a quick refresher, you can find more information [here](../rancher/quotas).

After clicking the "Submit" button, your secrets are generated and saved to your selected namespace. You can use the Rancher UI to access your secrets and view their contents.

## What to use the secrets for

For example, you can use the Samba secret when launching a MATLAB application to use your home directory on `storage-brno12-cerit.metacentrum.cz` as persistent storage.
