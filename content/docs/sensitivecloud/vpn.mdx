A Virtual Private Network (VPN) allows secure access to environments that are isolated from the public internet. At CERIT-SC, we use WireGuard VPN technology. To begin using the VPN, you must first request access and receive the necessary configuration details.

Please refer to the next section for instructions on how to obtain access.

To gain access to secure resources via Sensitive Cloud VPN, contact our support team at:  
[trusted(at)e-infra.cz](mailto:trusted@e-infra.cz). By default, the VPN profile comes together with a project allocation in compute container cloud or storage.

## Connecting to the VPN with Sensitive Cloud VPN

1. **Install WireGuard**  
   Download the WireGuard installer for your prefered platform [here](https://www.eduvpn.org/client-apps/) and follow the installation instructions.

2. Run the `eduVPN Client` on your computer.
3. Enter the insitution to connect with: `vpn.trusted.e-infra.cz`
  ![ev1](/img/ceritsc/eduvpn1.png)
3. After selecting `vpn.trusted.e-infra.cz`, you will be redirected to the browser to login to your e-INFRA CZ account. You will have to authorize your client to establish the connection to the Sensitive Cloud VPN.
   ![ev2](/img/ceritsc/eduvpn2.png)
4. Toggle switch to establish the connection and done!
   ![ev3](/img/ceritsc/eduvpn3.png)

## About the Sensitive Cloud VPN
- Your IP Address might change - it's not a static lease of IP address for your client.
- You might use as many devices as you want with concurent use of VPN. 
- Each Sensitive Cloud VPN project (bound to compute or storage project) supports up to 128 concurent connections*

* - exceptions might apply for bigger research groups

## Machine-to-Machine access?
In case you need the static lease of IP address for VPN tunneling to Sensitive Cloud, it is possible to hand static `Wireguard` connection profile. This can be arranged upon your request at e-mail [trusted@e-infra.cz](mailto:trusted@e-infra.cz).
