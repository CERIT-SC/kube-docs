## Running phpMyAdmin

If you are not already logged in, log in to [rancher.cloud.e-infra.cz](https://rancher.cloud.e-infra.cz). For more information, see the [Rancher](../rancher/rancher) section.

This article guides you through installing the phpMyAdmin application. phpMyAdmin is a web-based tool for administering MySQL databases, supporting a wide range of operations on both MySQL and MariaDB. 

## Select application to run

Navigate through `Only User Namespaces` (1), `App & Marketplace` (2), `Charts` (3), `cerit-sc` (4), and select `phpmyadmin` (5). Refer to the screenshot below for guidance.

![Select phpMyAdmin application](/img/ceritsc/apps/phpmyadmin/select.png)

## Install the application

Click on the chart, then click `Install` to continue.

Select your namespace and enter a name for your application, then click `Next`.

![Select namespace and name](/img/ceritsc/apps/phpmyadmin/namespace.png)

You will see two customization options. In the `Port` field, enter your database port and click `Install`.

![Set database port](/img/ceritsc/apps/phpmyadmin/port.png)

Wait for the success message to appear.

![Installation success message](/img/ceritsc/apps/phpmyadmin/success.png)

Navigate to `Service Discovery`, then `Ingresses`, and click on your hostname.

![Navigate to Ingress](/img/ceritsc/apps/phpmyadmin/ingress.png)

You will see the application login screen. Enter your database login credentials. For this tutorial, we are logging into Moodle's MariaDB database.

In the `Server` field, enter your database service name. In the `Username` field, enter your database username, then enter your password.

![Database login screen](/img/ceritsc/apps/phpmyadmin/login.png)

After a successful login, you will be greeted with this window:

![phpMyAdmin main interface](/img/ceritsc/apps/phpmyadmin/end.png)

## Uninstalling the application

To uninstall the application, navigate to `App & Marketplace`, then `Installed Apps`, select your instance, click `Delete`, and wait for the confirmation message to appear.

![Uninstall application](/img/ceritsc/apps/phpmyadmin/uninstall.png)
