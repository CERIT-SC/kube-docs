## Introduction

**Matrix** is an open, secure, and decentralized communication platform designed for modern collaboration. It enables you to:

- Chat with individuals or groups
- Join public or private rooms
- Share files, images, and links
- Communicate securely using end-to-end encryption (E2EE)

This guide walks you through using **Element**, the official Matrix client, provisioned on-premise on the e-INFRA CZ infrastructure.

## Welcome Screen & Initial Setup

When signing in for the first time, you will see the welcome screen and be guided through two initial setup tasks before accessing your messages:

1. **Encryption setup**
2. **Notification settings**



### Setting Up Encryption

Matrix uses **End-to-End Encryption (E2EE)** to secure your messages.

- Only conversation participants can read messages.
- Server administrators cannot inspect your encrypted communication.
- Your chats remain completely private.

To start configuring key backup, click **Continue**.

![Encryption setup welcome](/img/ceritsc/matrix-welcome.png)


#### Get Your Recovery Key

Matrix will generate a unique **Recovery Key**.

![Encryption setup](/img/ceritsc/matrix-encryption.png)

![Get recovery key](/img/ceritsc/matrix-get-key.png)

This key is essential to:
- Restore encrypted message history.
- Verify new devices.
- Recover access if you log out on all sessions.

<Callout type="warn">
⚠️ **Important:** The Recovery Key is displayed **only once**.
</Callout>

#### Save Your Recovery Key

Store your key in a safe, persistent location. Recommended options:

- A secure password manager
- An encrypted file store
- A printed physical copy stored securely

![Save recovery key](/img/ceritsc/matrix-save-key.png)

<Callout type="warn">
⚠️ If you lose your Recovery Key and have no other verified active devices, you may permanently lose access to your encrypted message history.
</Callout>


#### Verify via Recovery Key

When signing in on a new device, Matrix will ask for this key to unlock your historical chats.

- Enter or paste your **Recovery Key**.
- Click `Finish set up`.

![Enter recovery key](/img/ceritsc/matrix-enter-key.png)

Once entered, your encrypted conversations will be decrypted and available on the new device.


### Notifications Setup

Enable notifications to receive real-time alerts for:
- Direct messages

- New activity in active rooms

## Joining Public Rooms

Matrix hosts central public channels. Two main rooms are available for discussion regarding the infrastructure's LLM services:

1. **LLM - Status** (System updates, releases, and outages)
2. **LLM - General** (Open discussion on LLM topics and usage)

### How to Find and Join Public Rooms:

1. Click **Explore Rooms** (Room Directory).
2. Enter a keyword (e.g., `LLM`) into the search bar.
3. Browse the room directory list.
4. Click **View** to preview a room.

![Search rooms](/img/ceritsc/matrix-searchrooms.png)

5. Click **Join the discussion** to become a member.

![Join room](/img/ceritsc/matrix-add2room.png)

Joined rooms will now remain saved on your sidebar home interface for quick access.

![Home page with rooms](/img/ceritsc/matrix-home2.png)

<Callout type="note">
Private or moderated rooms may require administrator approval before access is granted.
</Callout>


### Inviting Members to a Room

If you have permission to invite members to a specific room:

1. Open the designated room.
2. Open the **Room Information** panel (top right).
3. Select **Members**.
4. Click **Invite**.
5. Search for the user (must be a registered Matrix user).
6. Send the invitation.

The invited user will receive an in-app notification to accept or decline the room invitation.

<Callout type="warning">
⚠️ **E-mail invitations are not supported**.
Inviting external users via email requires third-party plugins that transmit internal data outside our infrastructure, which is disabled to comply with e-INFRA CZ security policies. Users must first register their account before they can be invited or found in search.
</Callout>


## Mobile Setup: Element X App

Access your e-INFRA CZ Matrix account on mobile (iOS/Android) using **Element X**, the modern and lightweight Matrix client.

### Step 1: Install the App
Download **Element X** from your official store:
- **iOS:** [App Store](https://apps.apple.com) — Search for `Element X`
- **Android:** [Google Play](https://play.google.com) — Search for `Element X`

### Step 2: Change the Server (Important)
By default, Element X connects to public `matrix.org`. Point the client to the local e-INFRA CZ server:

1. On the welcome screen, tap **Change Server** (or ** Choose Server**).
2. Enter the custom server URL: `matrix.cloud.e-infra.cz`
3. Tap **Continue** or **Confirm**.

### Step 3: Single Sign-On (SSO) Login
Authenticate through your mobile browser using your **eduID.cz** or **e-INFRA CZ password** credentials.

### Step 4: Verify Your Mobile Device
To read encrypted history on your phone, verify your session using one of the following methods:

- **Option A (Emoji Verification):** Compare the sequence of emojis displayed on both your computer screen and mobile device. If they match, tap **Confirm** on both devices.
- **Option B (Recovery Key):** If you don't have an active web session open on another device, select **Use Recovery Key** and enter your saved key.

<Callout type="note">
**Device Verification Note:** QR code verification is not supported on our infrastructure. Please use Emoji Match or your Recovery Key to verify new devices.
</Callout>

## Quick Security & Best Practices Summary

- **Safeguard your Recovery Key:** Keep it stored safely in a password manager. Loss of this key means losing historical encrypted messages.
- **Configure Notifications:** Ensure device notifications are active so you don't miss important user mentions or room invites.
- **Use Direct Messages vs. Rooms:** Use DMs for private 1-on-1 chats and Rooms for group projects and topic discussion.
- **Verify Devices:** Always verify newly logged-in mobile or browser sessions to ensure continuous end-to-end encryption integrity.
