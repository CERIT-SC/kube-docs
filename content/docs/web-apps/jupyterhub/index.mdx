**JupyterHub** at CERIT-SC provides researchers and scientists with instant, browser-based access to interactive computing environments (JupyterLab, RStudio, MATLAB, and VS Code) backed by high-performance CPU and GPU resources.

To accelerate your research, our platform features **built-in, on-premise Generative AI assistants** (Jupyter AI, Claude Code, and Posit Assistant). Because our Large Language Models (LLMs) run entirely on CERIT-SC infrastructure, your unpublished research data, sensitive datasets, and proprietary code never leave our servers.

To access JupyterHub, you must have an active **MetaCentrum** account. Log in at **[hub.cloud.e-infra.cz](https://hub.cloud.e-infra.cz/)**.

## Quick Start: Launching Your Environment

1. **Quick Launch:** Click **`My Server`** to start a default environment immediately.
2. **Custom Launch (Recommended):** To run multiple environments or customize your setup, click **`+ New Server`**, enter a short, descriptive name for your server (up to 30 characters), and click **`Add Server`** to configure your instance.

You can run up to **15 servers** at the same time (the hub home page shows your current count). Each stopped server card also has a **`Quick Start`** button that relaunches it with its last configuration without going through the full form.

When spawning a new server, you will configure three main components: your **Software Image**, **Storage**, and **Hardware Resources**. The following pages walk you through each task:

- **[Start a Server](./jupyterhub/start-server)** — pick the right software image, storage, and CPU/RAM/GPU resources for your workload.
- **[Manage Servers](./jupyterhub/manage-servers)** — stop, delete, and add servers, and share a live notebook with collaborators.
- **[AI Assistants](./jupyterhub/ai-assistants)** — use Jupyter AI, Claude Code, and Posit Assistant on our on-premise LLM infrastructure.
- **[Custom Environments](./jupyterhub/custom-environments)** — create persistent conda environments and build your own Docker images.
- **[SSH Access](./jupyterhub/ssh-access)** — connect to your notebook with local VS Code or PyCharm over SSH.
- **[Expose a Web App](./jupyterhub/expose-apps)** — give your Streamlit / Dash / Gradio app a public URL.
- **[Run a Background Job](./jupyterhub/jobs)** — offload long or heavy computations from your interactive notebook.
- **[Server Policies](./jupyterhub/idle-shutdown)** — automatic idle shutdown rules and how to keep your server alive.
- **[Troubleshooting](./jupyterhub/troubleshooting)** — common spawn errors and their fixes.
