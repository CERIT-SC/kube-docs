## Introduction

DeepSite is a web-based smart web page developer who can build exactly what you describe by the words. Create actual, functioning websites just by describing what do you want. No code. No design skills. Just your imagination and some clear instructions.

Under the hood, it's powered by DeepSeek V1 – a seriously sophisticated AI that turns your casual requests like "Make me a project web site with a dark theme and scrolling animations" into actual working code. HTML, CSS, JavaScript – the whole package gets generated behind the scenes while you sip your coffee.

Unlike public cloud-hosted AI services, DeepSite runs **entirely on MetaCenter infrastructure**, ensuring data security, privacy, and performance optimized for our environment.

- Interact with the **Deepseek V1 model** running on Kubernetes.
- Simply improve and correct proposals using the **Qwen3-coder model**.
- Access AI resources securely through your **MetaCenter account**.

## Main Interface

The DeepSite application provides a clean and intuitive interface. The main sections are:

### 1. Navigation Panel
- **Dashboard** – overview of your recent sessions and system status.
- **Chat** – primary interface to interact with the Deepseek V1 model.
- **Corrections** – view and apply improvements generated by the Qwen3-coder model.
- **Settings** – manage preferences such as model parameters and interface options.

### 2. Chat Window
- **Input Box** – type your questions, prompts, or code.
- **Send Button** – submit your query to the Deepseek V1 model.
- **Response Area** – displays AI-generated outputs.

### 3. Correction Suggestions
- When applicable, proposals are automatically analyzed by **Qwen3-coder**.
- Suggestions appear in a sidebar or overlay.
- You can choose to **accept**, **reject**, or **compare** the suggestions.

---

## Workflow Example

1. **Start a new chat** by selecting *Chat* from the navigation panel.  
2. **Enter your query** (e.g., request for text generation, summarization, or code).  
3. **Review the Deepseek V1 response** in the output window.  
4. If corrections are detected, switch to the **Corrections** tab:  
- Compare the original response with the improved version.  
- Accept the correction if it fits your needs.  
5. **Save, download or copy** the final output for your project.  

---

## Settings & Customization

You can adjust several options in **Settings**:

- **Response Length** – control maximum output size.
- **Temperature** – adjust creativity vs. determinism of the model.
- **Correction Mode** – enable or disable automatic Qwen3-coder corrections.
- **Theme** – switch between light and dark modes.

---

## Best Practices

- **Be precise in your prompts** – clearer inputs lead to better outputs.
- **Use corrections wisely** – Qwen3-coder is designed for refining, not rewriting your intent.
- **Save important outputs** – DeepSite sessions are not stored permanently.
- **Respect resource limits** – MetaCenter infrastructure is shared among multiple users.
