## Introduction

📅 **[polls.e-infra.cz](https://polls.e-infra.cz)** is a scheduling platform powered by **[Rallly](https://rallly.co)**, an open-source tool for easily coordinating meeting times across groups. It is designed to help people find the best time to meet by **voting on date/time options**. 

It’s ideal for:
* Coordinating meetings across research teams
* Scheduling interviews, calls, or collaborative sessions
* Avoiding lengthy email threads about availability

This instance of Rallly is self-hosted by **CERIT-SC Kubernetes** using the official [open-source version of Rallly](https://github.com/lukevella/rallly), deployed via Docker.

### Key Features

- ✅ **Sign-in required for poll creation** – To create a poll, you must be signed in using your **e-INFRA CZ account**.
- ✅ **Voting without an account** – Participants do **not** need an account to vote.
- ✅ **Anonymous voting allowed** – Voters can participate without logging in, but must provide a **name** and optionally an **email address**
- ✅ **Customizable poll settings** – When creating a poll, you can choose whether providing an **email address is required** for participants.
- ✅ **Email updates for organizers** – Organizers receive **e-mail notifications when someone votes**.
- ✅ **Finalized meeting = calendar invite** – When the poll is closed, participants automatically receive an **email notification** and a **calendar invitation** (iCal), so no one can say they “missed it” next time. 😉
- ✅ **Privacy-respecting** – Hosted by e-INFRA CZ, no third-party tracking.
- ✅ **Free to use** – No limits on the number of polls or participants.


## Accessing Polls

Open-WebUI is accessible at https://polls.e-infra.cz. To use the full potential of the platform, you need a valid [e-INFRA CZ account](https://docs.account.e-infra.cz/en/docs/access/account).

## How Does It Work?

A scheduling poll lets you propose one or more **date/time options** for a meeting. Others can then vote on their availability.

![polls](/img/ceritsc/apps/polls/polls-month-view.png)

### Example workflow:
1. **Go to** [polls.e-infra.cz](https://polls.e-infra.cz)
2. Sign in with your **e-INFRA CZ account** to create a poll
3. Click **"Create a Poll"**
4. Fill in the event name and your name/email (optional)
5. Choose potential **date and time slots**
6. Share the poll link with participants
7. Wait for votes and choose the most suitable time
8. **Close the poll** — this sends out **calendar invites** and a **confirmation email**


👉 See full workflow documentation here: 📖 [Rallly Poll Creation Guide](https://support.rallly.co/workflow/create)


## Guest Users vs. Registered Users

<Callout type="info">
**Note:** Polls created without an account are not possible on `polls.e-infra.cz`. Only logged-in users may create polls.
</Callout>

- 🧑‍💼 **Poll creators must be logged in** with an e-INFRA CZ identity.
- 🧑 **Voters do not need an account**, but they are required to enter at least a **name**, and optionally an **e-mail address**.
- ✏️ When creating a poll, you can configure whether e-mail is **required** from participants.
