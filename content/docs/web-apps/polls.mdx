## Polls Introduction

📅 **[polls.e-infra.cz](https://polls.e-infra.cz)** is a scheduling platform powered by **[Rallly](https://rallly.co)**, an open-source tool for easily coordinating meeting times across groups. It is designed to help people find the best time to meet by **voting on date/time options**. 

It’s ideal for:
* Coordinating meetings across research teams
* Scheduling interviews, calls, or collaborative sessions
* Avoiding lengthy email threads about availability

This instance of Rallly is self-hosted by **CERIT-SC Kubernetes** using the official [open-source version of Rallly](https://github.com/lukevella/rallly), deployed via Docker.

### Key Features

- ✅ **No login required** – Create or vote on polls without an account.
- ✅ **Simple & intuitive UI** – Schedule in just a few clicks.
- ✅ **Privacy-respecting** – Hosted by e-INFRA CZ, no third-party tracking.
- ✅ **Free to use** – No limits on the number of polls or participants.


## Accessing Polls

Open-WebUI is accessible at https://polls.e-infra.cz. To use the full potential of the platform, you need the valid [e-INFRA CZ account](https://docs.account.e-infra.cz/en/docs/access/account).

## How Does It Work?

A scheduling poll lets you propose one or more **date/time options** for a meeting. Others can then vote on their availability.

### Example workflow:
1. **Go to** [polls.e-infra.cz](https://polls.e-infra.cz)
2. Click **"Create a Poll"**
3. Fill in the event name and your name/email (optional)
4. Choose potential **date and time slots**
5. Share the poll link with participants
6. Wait for votes and choose the most suitable time
7. **Close the poll** — this sends out **calendar invites** and a **confirmation email**


👉 See full workflow documentation here:  
📖 [Rallly Poll Creation Guide](https://support.rallly.co/workflow/create)


## Guest Users vs. Registered Users

You can create and manage polls without creating an account. However, **signing up is recommended** if you want to:

* Edit or delete your polls later
* Get email notifications about votes or comments
* Access your full poll history

<Callout type="warning">
**Note:** Polls created without an account **may be lost** if you lose the original poll link.
</Callout>
