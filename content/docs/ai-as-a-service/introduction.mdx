## AI for Science. Built for Researchers. Run securely in e-INFRA CZ.

CERIT-SC provides AI services hosted within the national research
infrastructure e-INFRA CZ. You can use them through a web browser,
connect them to your research software, or integrate them into your
coding environment.

The platform provides large language models (LLMs) for working with
text and code, models that can process images, and embedding models
that represent text numerically for search and document retrieval.
Available capabilities depend on the model and the service you use.

## Get access

AI chat and API access are available to users with an active MetaCentrum
account. Masaryk University students and staff can also use the
university registration route described in the
[access instructions](./chat-ai#accessing-open-webui).

The university registration route provides access to AI chat and API
services. Other platform services may require a MetaCentrum account
or additional setup.

1. **Register or check your account.**
   Follow the [access instructions](./chat-ai#accessing-open-webui)
   for your institution.

2. **Start with a small task.**
   Open [Chat AI](https://chat.ai.e-infra.cz), sign in through
   e-INFRA CZ, and select a model. For example, ask it to explain
   a short piece of code or revise a paragraph.

3. **Connect your tools when needed.**
   Follow the [API guide](./ai-api) or
   [coding assistant guide](./llm-integration) to use the service
   in your own working environment.

Before submitting research data, read the data-handling guidance below.

## Choose a model

Models differ in their capabilities, response time, and resource needs.
Start with a model suited to your task and evaluate its output on a
small, representative sample.

- **Text and code models** support tasks such as drafting, classification,
  information extraction, and programming assistance.
- **Multimodal models** can also process images, where supported.
- **Embedding models** convert text into numerical representations
  for semantic search and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).
  RAG supplies retrieved documents as context for a model's answer.

See the [model catalogue](./chat-ai#currently-available-models)
for capabilities and the
[live status page](https://llm.ai.e-infra.cz/status/)
for current availability.

### Model changes and reproducibility

Model versions may be upgraded or retired. A specific version is not
guaranteed to remain available throughout a research project.

Maintained [model aliases](./chat-ai#model-aliases), such as `mini`
or `thinker`, provide names that can continue to be used when the
underlying model changes. An alias does not preserve the model's
behaviour or guarantee identical results.

For work that contributes to a publication or other research output:

- Record the resolved model identifier and available model metadata,
  rather than only the alias.
- Save prompts, relevant input data, generation settings, dates,
  and outputs in accordance with your data-management requirements.
- Validate the workflow again after a model change.

The API guide explains how to
[retrieve model metadata](./ai-api#getting-model-metadata-via-the-api).
If your project depends on a specific model version, contact
[k8s@cerit-sc.cz](mailto:k8s@cerit-sc.cz) before starting.

## Research data and privacy

Standard hosted models run within e-INFRA CZ. External search tools
and separately arranged third-party services can involve sending
information outside this infrastructure.

Data handling also depends on how you access the service:

- **Chat interface:** conversations and uploaded files are stored
  by the service. Review the retention and deletion information
  before uploading documents.
- **Direct API:** the documented API route bypasses the chat interface's
  conversation storage. This does not, by itself, establish that a
  workflow meets your project's data-protection requirements.

The chat interface is intended for general research assistance and
is not certified for protected data. Before using sensitive,
personal, or otherwise restricted data, contact
[k8s@cerit-sc.cz](mailto:k8s@cerit-sc.cz) to discuss a suitable setup.

Read the [AI data privacy policy](./ai-data-privacy) for details
on external services, storage, logging, and retention.

## Use AI outputs in research

Treat model responses as material to evaluate. Check factual claims
and references against original sources, test generated code,
and verify calculations and extracted data.

For document-based questions, check that the cited passage supports
the answer. Supplying a document does not guarantee a correct response.

Before applying an automated workflow to a large dataset, assess its
quality on representative examples and define how errors will be
identified and handled.

## Share computing resources responsibly

The platform serves many researchers on shared computing resources.
Response times and availability can vary with demand.

- **Choose an appropriate model.** Start with a smaller or faster model
  for routine tasks, and use more demanding models when evaluation
  shows a benefit.
- **Limit concurrent requests.** Keep batch workloads controlled
  and respect service rate limits.
- **Use stable model selections.** Do not automatically switch models
  in response to short-term utilisation figures on the status page.
- **Coordinate independent benchmarks in advance.** Contact the
  support team before running them, as required by the
  [Chat AI guide](./chat-ai).
- **Plan large workloads.** Schedule substantial batch processing
  outside peak hours where possible, and discuss unusually demanding
  workloads with the support team.

You can review your API token consumption in the
[usage dashboard](https://llm.ai.e-infra.cz/usage/).
This dashboard does not include chat-interface usage.
