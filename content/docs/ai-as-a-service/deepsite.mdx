## Introduction

**DeepSite** is a web-based smart **AI web page (apps) generator** that proposes fully functional websites directly from your descriptions. No coding, no design expertise required—just describe what you want, and DeepSite will create it for you.

Powered by **DeepSeek R1**, a state-of-the-art AI, DeepSite can turn requests like *“Make me a project website with a dark theme and scrolling animations...”* into complete, working code (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and more). Everything happens automatically in the background—while you focus on your ideas.

Unlike public cloud-hosted AI services, DeepSite runs **exclusively on e-INFRA CZ / CERIT-SC infrastructure**, ensuring:
- **Data security and privacy**
- **Performance optimized for our environment**
- Access securely through your **MetaCenter account**.

## Logging In

1. Open your browser and go to [https://deepsite.ai.e-infra.cz/](https://deepsite.ai.e-infra.cz/).  
   *(Latest Chrome, Safari, or Edge recommended.)*  
2. Log in using **e-INFRA CZ**:  
   - Choose your home institution, or  
   - Use the `e-INFRA CZ password` option and log in via MetaCenter login and password.  
3. After login, you’ll be redirected to the **DeepSite Dashboard**.

## Example Workflow 

1. Provide a natural-language description of what you want. (e.g., *“Build a personal blog with a minimal layout and contact form”*).  Or supply reference links or examples of existing sites/apps you like. Provide reference links or screenshots if you have a design in mind.
2. Review the AI-generated output in the **Preview Panel**.  

<div class="w-120">
<img src="/img/ceritsc/deepsite-preview.png" alt="Preview" />
</div>

3. If needed, apply **corrections or refinements** in the chat.  
4. Your project is saved automatically.  



## Best Practices

- **Be specific in your prompts** → Clearer instructions produce better websites.  
- **Use refinements strategically** → Qwen3-coder is great for polishing, not redefining your intent.  
- **Save your work regularly** → Sessions are temporary and not stored by default.  
- **Be mindful of resources** → MetaCenter’s infrastructure is shared among users.
