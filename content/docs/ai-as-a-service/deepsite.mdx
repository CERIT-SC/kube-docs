## Introduction

**DeepSite** is a web-based **AI web page and application generator** that creates fully functional websites directly from your descriptions. No coding or design expertise is required—simply describe what you want, and DeepSite will build it for you.

Powered by **DeepSeek V3.2**, a state-of-the-art AI, DeepSite transforms requests such as *"Make me a project website with a dark theme and scrolling animations..."* into complete, working code (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and more). The entire process runs automatically in the background, allowing you to focus on your ideas.

Unlike public cloud-hosted AI services, DeepSite runs **exclusively on the e-INFRA CZ / CERIT-SC infrastructure**, ensuring:
- **Data security and privacy**
- **Performance optimized for our environment**
- Secure access through your **MetaCentrum account**.

## Logging In

1. Open your browser and go to [https://deepsite.ai.e-infra.cz/](https://deepsite.ai.e-infra.cz/).
   *(We recommend using the latest version of Chrome, Safari, or Edge.)*
2. Log in using **e-INFRA CZ**:
   - Choose your home institution, or
   - Select the `e-INFRA CZ password` option and enter your MetaCentrum login credentials.
3. After logging in, you will be redirected to the **DeepSite Dashboard**.

DeepSite provides a clean, intuitive workspace divided into several key sections:

1. **Code Editor** – Displays generated HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
2. **Chat Window** – Your primary interaction point. Use it to:
   - Enter prompts for new websites
   - Redesign existing pages

## Example Workflow

1. Provide a natural-language description of what you want. For example: *"Build a personal blog with a minimal layout and contact form."* Alternatively, supply reference links or examples of existing sites/apps you like.
2. Review the AI-generated output in the **Preview Panel**.

<div class="w-120">
<img src="/img/ceritsc/deepsite-preview.png" alt="Preview" />
</div>

3. If needed, apply **corrections or refinements** in the chat.
4. Your project is saved automatically.



## Best Practices

- **Be specific in your prompts** – Clearer instructions produce better websites.
- **Use refinements strategically** – `qwen3-coder` excels at polishing, not redefining your intent.
- **Save your work regularly** – Sessions are temporary and not stored by default.
- **Be mindful of resources** – MetaCentrum's infrastructure is shared among users.
