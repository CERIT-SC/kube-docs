# Sending VO invitation
First you need to login into [Perun system](https://perun.einfra.cesnet.cz/).

After logging in you need to choose **Access management (1)** in the left menu. Then you can insert the **name of your VO (2)** or you can straight **click on your VO (3)** in the list below.

![perun](/img/data-care/perun/perun-invite1.png)

In the detail of your VO, you need to click on the **User (1)** tile.

![perun](/img/data-care/perun/perun-invite2.png)

Then in the following page, you need to click on the **Invite (1)** button.

![perun](/img/data-care/perun/perun-invite3.png)

The next step is to fill in the `Name` and `Email` field and then select the `Language` option. In the end, you can click on the **Invite (1)** button. Once you click on the **Invite (1)** button the notification will be sent to the desired email address.

![perun](/img/data-care/perun/perun-invite4.png)

Example of the notification is listed below.

```text
Dear user,

administrator of Virtual Organization (VO) VO_xyz invites you to use data space in this VO. To become a member of VO, you must fill in an application. You must authenticate yourself using following link:

Invitation URL

You can see your application state in "Applications" section at:

Applications URL

You will be notified by another mail once your application approved.

--------------------------------------------------
Administrator VO_xyz
du-support@cesnet.cz
https://docs.du.cesnet.cz/
```
